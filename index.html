ABOUT ME

Hi, I’m Keighan Kyler Haftl. I’m a digital artist and coder who loves bringing creative ideas to life on screen. For me, art and code aren't separate worlds—they're just two different ways of building something awesome from scratch.
Digital art is where I spend most of my time. Whether I’m designing characters or drawing turnarounds, I love making ideas feel real. I don't stop at 2D, though. I've also worked with animation, traditional painting, and 3D sculpting, which really helped me figure out how light, form, and movement work together.
On the coding side, Python is my go-to language. But I’ve also built projects with HTML/CSS, Java, and C# in Unity, alongside experimenting with modern vibe coding tools to bring prototypes to life quickly. I like getting my hands dirty with physical hardware, too—mostly building robotics projects using Raspberry Pi and wiring up different sensors.
A big highlight for me was traveling to Kyoto for the CIEE Game Design Camp. Getting to dive deep into game mechanics, art direction, and coding in a place so rich in game culture completely changed how I think about interactive design. I’m really excited to bring all of this—the art, the logic, and the curiosity—into a university environment where I can keep experimenting and growing.
