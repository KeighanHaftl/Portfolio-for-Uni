OTHER CODE PROJECTS & DEMOS

Quiz

Program: Unity
Language: C#
Function: It generates a random calculation by randomly selecting two numbers between 1 and 20 as well as a random number between 1 and 3 to determine the type of calculation (addition, subtraction, or multiplication), then 4 numbers are applied to the 4 buttons, one being the correct answer, the others being the wrong answers that are a minimum of –10 away or maximum 10 away. If the correct option was chosen, the button turns green and you get a point. If the answer was wrong, the answer you picked would become red and the correct answer would become green. At the end, the game will should how many points you got.

Calculator

Program: Unity
Language: C#
Function: It handles all your basic math needs like addition, subtraction, multiplication, division, percentages, and a function to make positive numbers negative and vice versa. The display updates instantly as you type, and it includes quick controls to clear your work or fix mistakes on the fly.

Weather App

Program: Unity
Language: C#
Function: This weather app allows users to select a country and city. Then, with the help of a Weather API, the app shows them the location, the temperature, the humidity, the condition, the time and the AQi.

ROBOTICS CAMP

Robotics Camp Demo

A short demonstration and explaination of the robotics project in action. Over a 3-week project, a teammate and I built a mini delivery robot designed to follow a track and drop off packages safely. I handled most of the coding on a Raspberry Pi—setting up infrared sensors so it could track lines and ultrasonic sensors to keep it from bumping into things. We swapped roles whenever needed, so I also got to jump in and help with the wiring and building side of things.
